What counts as a hidden character, and what this cannot find

Five categories of characters that render as nothing, or as an ordinary space, while occupying real bytes: zero-width characters, bidirectional controls, variation selectors, nonstandard whitespace, and the Unicode Tags block. People use that gap for text watermarking, for steganographic payloads, and for Trojan-Source attacks (CVE-2021-42574).

What this cannot find. Statistical watermarks, the kind that bias which ordinary word a model picks, add no characters at all. Nothing in the bytes can reveal them, so no scanner can find them, this one included. The watermark Anthropic began shipping in Claude models in August 2026 is that kind. The narrow no-break space reported in ChatGPT output in 2025 is the other kind, and it is in scope here.

A clean result means "no hidden characters." It does not mean "not AI-generated."
